📐 Analysis Methodology
1️⃣
Data Collection
Player-level match data from 6 domestic leagues: IPL, SA20, BBL, CPL, ILT20, Ranji Trophy. 1,800 match records across 30 players, covering batting, bowling, and fielding stats.
2️⃣
Pressure Situation Tagging
Each match is tagged as a pressure scenario:
• Death-over batting (overs 17–20)
• Powerplay chase
• Knockout matches
• Last-5-needed-20+ situations
• Top-order collapse (3+ wkts in 10 overs)
3️⃣
Pressure Index Formula
Batting PI = 0.6 × (Pressure Avg / Normal Avg) + 0.4 × (Pressure SR / Normal SR)

Bowling PI = Normal Economy / Pressure Economy

Win Lift = Win% under pressure − Win% in normal matches
4️⃣
Composite Pressure Score
Role-specific weighted average (0–100 scale):

Batsmen/WK: 40% Bat PI + 10% Bowl PI + 20% Win Lift + 20% Knockout Perf + 10% Volume

Bowlers: 15% Bat PI + 45% Bowl PI + 20% Win Lift + 10% Knockout Perf + 10% Volume

All-Rounders: 28% Bat + 28% Bowl + 20% Win + 14% Knockout + 10% Volume
📌 Key Insight
A player's raw stats don't reveal clutch potential. What separates an elite IPL 2027 auction target is the delta between normal and pressure performance. Players with Pressure Index > 75 consistently elevate their game when the team needs it most — these are the match-winners RR should prioritise at the auction table.

The model further weights knockout performance and win contribution because pressure in a final or semi-final is fundamentally different from a league stage game.
